Cooking ideas

Not sure what to make? Start here.

Whole chicken

Roast it with garlic, lemon and paprika for Sunday lunch. Or cut it up for a big pot of curry. Use the leftovers for sandwiches and the carcass for stock.

Meaty bones

Simmer them with vegetables for a thick soup, or slow-cook with beans or potatoes. Cheap, filling and full of flavour.

Breast fillets

Slice for a quick stir-fry, grill with peri-peri, or fill wraps and rolls. Cooks fast, so it’s good for busy weeknights.

Always cook chicken right through until there’s no pink and the juices run clear.
